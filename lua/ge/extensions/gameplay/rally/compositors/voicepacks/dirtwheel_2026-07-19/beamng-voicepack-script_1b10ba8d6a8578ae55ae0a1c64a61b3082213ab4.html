Recording tips
  • Record in a quiet room. Keep a consistent distance from the mic.
  • Keep tone and energy consistent across the whole script so phrases blend together.
  • Deliver phrases like a real co-driver.
  • You can do as many takes as you want for each phrase. If you stumble mid-phrase, just try again.
  • Leave a bit of silence before and after each phrase for clean trimming.
  • Name each clip exactly as the displayed target filename.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Left/Right arrows move between phrases. Home/End jump to first/last.
